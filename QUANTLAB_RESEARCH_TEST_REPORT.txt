NOT VERIFIED / LIMITATIONS

- Phillips-Perron was not independently cross-checked against a specialized PP package.
- MA/ARMA use CSS + Nelder-Mead, not exact maximum likelihood; parity with statsmodels/R/Stata is not claimed.
- Forecast uncertainty still excludes parameter uncertainty and conditional heteroskedasticity.
- Browser end-to-end automation was not completed in this environment. Manual smoke test in Live Server/hosting is still required.
- RV/BV/jump variation are high-frequency estimates and have not been adjusted for microstructure noise or session-specific sampling.
- Walk-forward evaluates a limited number of evenly spaced one-step checkpoints, not every bar; no spread, commission, slippage, latency or market impact is included.
