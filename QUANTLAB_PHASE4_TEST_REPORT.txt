5) Known limitations / manual steps
- Constant-mean equation only; no ARMA mean + GARCH.
- No Hessian, parameter standard errors, confidence intervals, skewed-t/GED, VaR/ES, or stress testing.
- EGARCH and TARCH multi-step forecasts use documented plug-in approximations.
- No validation yet on the user's raw XAUUSD M1 CSV or a multi-period out-of-sample market dataset.
- Browser end-to-end smoke test on Live Server and the deployed hosting must be completed manually.
- Phase 5 (VAR/VECM, Cointegration/Johansen, Granger, Bootstrap/Monte Carlo and advanced regime work) is not included in this package.
